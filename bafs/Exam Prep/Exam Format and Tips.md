# Exam Format and Tips

> [!abstract] Overview
> This is a school-level BAFS exam (Accounting Strand). One paper, 150 minutes. Understanding the format, question types, and time management strategies is essential for exam success.

---

## 1. Paper Structure

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Papers** | 1 (single paper) |
| **Duration** | 150 minutes (2 hours 30 minutes) |
| **Content** | Compulsory + Elective parts |

> [!important] Time Management
> - 150 minutes total
> - ~1.5 minutes per mark (assuming 100 marks)
> - Allocate time proportionally; don't spend too long on one question

---

## 2. Question Types

| Type | What It Tests | Tips |
|------|---------------|------|
| **Multiple-choice questions (MC)** | Broad coverage of syllabus | Eliminate wrong answers; watch for "NOT" or "EXCEPT" |
| **Short questions (SQ)** | Basic knowledge and essential concepts | Be precise; use key terminology |
| **Accounting problems** | Application of double entry, adjustments, financial statements | Show working; use proper format |
| **Business cases** | Comprehension and knowledge application | Read the case carefully; link to theory |
| **Essays** | In-depth discussion of open-ended issues | Structure: intro → arguments for/against → conclusion |

---

## 3. Exam Scope Focus

> [!exam] Key Exam Areas

> - **Ch3:** Stakeholders (MC & SQ) - Compulsory
> - **Ch11:** Depreciation (MC) - Compulsory
> - **Ch13-15:** Partnership (Question) - Financial Accounting
> - **Ch23:** Accounting Concepts (Question) - Financial Accounting
> - **Ch24-27:** Cost Accounting (MC & Question) - Cost Accounting

---

## 4. Time Allocation Strategy

| Section | Suggested Time | Focus |
|---------|----------------|-------|
| MC questions | 30-40 minutes | Quick, accurate; eliminate wrong answers |
| Short questions | 30-40 minutes | Be precise; use key terms |
| Accounting problems | 50-60 minutes | Show workings; check debits = credits |
| Essay/discussion | 20-30 minutes | Structure your answer clearly |

> [!tip] Time Management Tips
> - Start with questions you find easiest to build confidence
> - If stuck, move on and come back later
> - Leave 5-10 minutes at the end to check your work
> - Don't spend more than 2 minutes on any single MC question

---

## 5. Common Question Patterns

### Pattern 1: "Compare and Contrast"

Structure your answer:
1. Define both concepts
2. List similarities
3. List differences (use a table)
4. Give examples
5. Conclude with implications

### Pattern 2: "Discuss / Evaluate"

Structure your answer:
1. Introduction — define the issue
2. Arguments FOR (with reasons and examples)
3. Arguments AGAINST (with reasons and examples)
4. Your evaluation — balanced conclusion
5. Recommendation (if applicable)

### Pattern 3: "Calculate and Comment"

Structure your answer:
1. Show the formula
2. Substitute the numbers
3. Calculate the answer
4. Comment on what it means (higher/lower than expected, trend, etc.)
5. Suggest reasons and implications

### Pattern 4: Accounting Problem

Structure your answer:
1. Identify the required output (journal entries, T-accounts, financial statements)
2. Work systematically through each transaction/adjustment
3. Show all working clearly
4. Balance/total at the end
5. Check that debits = credits (for journal entries)

## 6. Revision Strategy

| Days Before Exam | Focus |
|------------------|-------|
| 1-2 weeks | Complete all topic revision; do practice questions |
| 3-5 days | Practice under timed conditions |
| 1-2 days | Review weak areas; re-do difficult questions |
| Day before | Quick review of formulas and key concepts; rest well |

> [!warning] Common Mistakes to Avoid
> - Not showing working in calculations
> - Forgetting period-end adjustments
> - Mixing up debit and credit rules
> - Not interpreting ratios (just calculating them)
> - Spending too long on one question
> - Not reading the question carefully (missing key requirements)
