## Ch3: Stakeholders (MC & SQ)

| Stakeholder | Interest | Conflict |
|-------------|----------|----------|
| Owners | Profit, ROI | vs Employee welfare |
| Employees | Job security, wages | vs Cost reduction |
| Customers | Quality, fair prices | vs Profit margins |
| Suppliers | Prompt payment | vs Cost cutting |
| Creditors | Timely repayment | vs Business expansion |
| Government | Tax, compliance | vs Profit minimisation |
| Community | Employment, environment | vs Industrial expansion |

**Key Debate:** Profit maximisation vs Stakeholder theory

## Ch13-15: Partnership (Question)

**Appropriation Account:**
```
Net profit
- Partner salaries
- Interest on capital
= Residual profit
÷ Profit-sharing ratio
```

**Current Account:**
- Dr: Drawings
- Cr: Share of profit, Salary, Interest on capital

**Changes:**
- Admission/Retirement → Revalue assets + Goodwill

## Ch24-27: Cost Accounting (MC & Question)

**Cost Types:**
- Fixed: Rent, insurance
- Variable: Materials, labour
- Sunk: NEVER relevant
- Opportunity: Benefit foregone

**Marginal vs Absorption:**
| | Marginal | Absorption |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed costs | Period costs | Product costs |
| Inventory | Variable only | Variable + fixed |
| Production > sales | Lower profit | Higher profit |

**CVP Formulas:**
- Contribution = SP − VC
- BEP (units) = FC ÷ Contribution
- Margin of safety = (Actual − BEP) ÷ Actual

## Ch11: Depreciation (MC)

**Methods:**
| Method | Formula |
|--------|---------|
| Straight-line | (Cost − Residual) ÷ Life |
| Reducing balance | NBV × Rate% |

**Key Points:**
- Dr Depreciation expense
- Cr Accumulated depreciation
- Capital expenditure → Asset → Depreciate
- Revenue expenditure → Expense immediately
- Inventory: Lower of cost and NRV

## Last Minute Tips

1. **MC:** Eliminate wrong answers first
2. **SQ:** Use bullet points, be concise
3. **Question:** Show workings, label answers
4. **Partnership:** Always show appropriation account
5. **CVP:** Draw diagram if unsure
6. **Depreciation:** Check capital vs revenue
7. **Concepts:** Link to scenario, name the principle
