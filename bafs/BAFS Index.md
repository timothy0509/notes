# BAFS Index

> [!abstract] Subject Overview
> Business, Accounting and Financial Studies (BAFS) — elective subject under the Technology Education Key Learning Area. This vault covers the **Accounting Strand** (Compulsory + Elective parts).

---

## Compulsory Part (~25%)

> [!info] Foundation Topics
> These three topics provide a broad-based foundation in business before progressing to the in-depth Accounting module.

| Topic | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| [[Business Environment]] | HK economy, forms of business ownership, business ethics |
| [[Basics of Management]] | Management functions, key business functions, SMEs |
| [[Personal Financial Management]] | Time value of money, consumer credit, investments, stock trading |

---

## Elective Part — Financial Accounting (~60%)

> [!tip] Core Accounting Module
> This is the largest and most heavily examined section. Master the accounting cycle, financial statements, and analysis.

| Topic | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| [[Purposes and Role of Accounting]] | Importance, functions, the accounting cycle |
| [[Double Entry System]] | Accounting equation, debit/credit rules |
| [[Books of Original Entry and Ledgers]] | Journals, ledgers, posting process |
| [[Trial Balance]] | Functions, limitations, preparation |
| [[Period-End Adjustments]] | Accruals, depreciation, inventory valuation |
| [[Financial Reporting by Business Ownership]] | Sole proprietorship, partnership, limited company accounts |
| [[Control System]] | Bank reconciliation, correction of errors |
| [[Incomplete Records]] | Statement of affairs, calculating profit from incomplete records |
| [[Accounting Principles and Conventions]] | Business entity, going concern, prudence, materiality, etc. |
| [[Financial Analysis and Ratios]] | Profitability, liquidity, solvency, efficiency, ROI ratios |

## Exam Scope Focus

> [!exam] Key Exam Areas
> **School Exam:** 150 minutes, one paper
> Based on the exam scope, prioritise these topics:
> - **Ch3:** Stakeholders (MC & SQ) - Compulsory
> - **Ch11:** Depreciation (MC) - Compulsory
> - **Ch13-15:** Partnership (Question) - Financial Accounting
> - **Ch23:** Accounting Concepts (Question) - Financial Accounting
> - **Ch24-27:** Cost Accounting (MC & Question) - Cost Accounting
