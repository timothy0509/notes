# Financial Reporting by Business Ownership

> [!abstract] Overview
> Financial statements are prepared differently depending on the form of business ownership. This topic covers the preparation of income statements and statements of financial position for sole proprietorships, partnerships, and limited companies.

### Goodwill

> [!note] Definition
> **Goodwill** is the value of a business's reputation, customer base, and other intangible factors that enable it to earn higher profits than a new business in the same industry.

**Factors affecting goodwill:**
- Quality of management
- Brand reputation
- Customer loyalty
- Location
- Profit track record

### Changes in Partnership

| Event | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| **Change in profit-sharing ratio** | Revalue assets, adjust capital accounts |
| **Admission of new partner** | Revalue assets, calculate goodwill, adjust capital |
| **Retirement of partner** | Revalue assets, calculate goodwill, adjust capital |

> [!example] Worked Example
> Partners A and B share profits 3:2. C is admitted for 1/4 share.
> Revaluation shows equipment has increased by \$10,000.
>
> Dr Equipment: \$10,000
> Cr Revaluation account: \$10,000
>
> Revaluation profit shared: A = \$6,000, B = \$4,000

## 3. Limited Company Accounts

### Share Capital

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Ordinary shares** | Residual claim on profits and assets |
| **Preference shares** | Fixed dividend, priority over ordinary on winding up |
| **Debentures (loan capital)** | Fixed interest, debt not equity |

### Journal Entries for Share Issue

**Ordinary shares issued at par:**

**Debentures issued:**
| Account | Dr | Cr |
|---------|----|----|
| Bank | X | |
| Debentures | | X |

### Reserves

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Revenue reserves** | Retained profits available for distribution |
| **Capital reserves** | Not available for dividend (share premium, revaluation surplus) |
| **Provisions** | Amounts set aside for specific purposes |

### Limited Company Income Statement

Same format as sole proprietorship but with:
- Interest on debentures deducted before tax
- Tax shown separately
- Dividends shown as appropriation of profit
