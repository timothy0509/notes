# Double Entry System

> [!abstract] Overview
> The double entry system is the foundation of modern accounting. Every transaction has **two effects** — a debit and a credit of equal amounts. This topic covers the accounting equation, rules of debit and credit, and how transactions affect the equation.

---

## 1. The Accounting Equation

$$\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Owner's Equity}$$

| Element | Definition | Examples |
|---------|------------|----------|
| **Assets** | Resources owned by the business | Cash, inventory, equipment, buildings |
| **Liabilities** | Obligations to outsiders | Loans, trade payables, overdrafts |
| **Owner's Equity** | Owner's claim on assets | Capital, retained profits |

> [!important] This equation MUST always balance. Every transaction affects at least two elements, keeping the equation in balance.

### Extended Equation

$$\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Capital} + \text{Revenue} - \text{Expenses} - \text{Drawings}$$

---

## 2. Rules of Debit and Credit

| Account Type | Increase | Decrease |
|--------------|----------|----------|
| **Assets** | Debit (Dr) | Credit (Cr) |
| **Liabilities** | Credit (Cr) | Debit (Dr) |
| **Owner's Equity** | Credit (Cr) | Debit (Dr) |
| **Revenue/Income** | Credit (Cr) | Debit (Dr) |
| **Expenses** | Debit (Dr) | Credit (Cr) |
| **Drawings** | Debit (Dr) | Credit (Cr) |

> [!tip] Memory Aid
> **DEA-LER**:
> - **D**ebit: **E**xpenses, **A**ssets, **L**osses (drawings)
> - **C**redit: **E**quity, **R**evenue (income), **G**ains

### T-Account Format

```
         Account Name
    ┌────────┬────────┐
    │  Debit │ Credit │
    │  (Dr)  │  (Cr)  │
    ├────────┼────────┤
    │        │        │
    │        │        │
    └────────┴────────┘
```

## 3. How Transactions Affect the Equation

| Transaction | Assets | = | Liabilities | + | Equity |
|-------------|--------|---|-------------|---|--------|
| Owner invests \$100,000 cash | +100,000 (Cash) | = | — | + | +100,000 (Capital) |
| Buy equipment \$20,000 cash | +20,000 (Equipment) −20,000 (Cash) | = | — | + | — |
| Purchase goods \$5,000 on credit | +5,000 (Inventory) | = | +5,000 (Payables) | + | — |
| Sell goods \$8,000 cash (cost \$5,000) | +8,000 (Cash) −5,000 (Inventory) | = | — | + | +3,000 (Revenue − COGS) |
| Pay rent \$2,000 | −2,000 (Cash) | = | — | + | −2,000 (Expense) |

> [!example] Worked Example
> **Transaction:** Owner starts business with \$50,000 cash.
>
> | Account | Dr | Cr |
> |---------|----|----|
> | Cash (Asset ↑) | 50,000 | |
> | Capital (Equity ↑) | | 50,000 |
>
> Equation: Assets \$50,000 = Liabilities \$0 + Equity \$50,000 ✓

## 4. Source Documents

|----------|---------|
