## Angles & Lines

### Angles on a Line & at a Point

| Ref | Statement |
|-----|-----------|
| Adj. ∠s on straight line | Adjacent angles on a straight line sum to $180^\circ$ |
| Vert. opp. ∠s | Vertically opposite angles are equal |
| ∠s at a point | Angles at a point sum to $360^\circ$ |

### Parallel Lines

| Ref | Statement |
|-----|-----------|
| Corresponding ∠s | If two lines are cut by a transversal, corresponding angles are equal |
| Alt. ∠s | Alternate interior angles are equal |
| Co-interior ∠s | Co-interior (allied) angles sum to $180^\circ$ |
| Parallel ⟹ equal slope | Two lines are parallel if and only if they have equal slopes |
| Perp. lines | Two lines are perpendicular if the product of their slopes is $-1$ |

### Polygons

| Ref | Statement |
|-----|-----------|
| ∠ sum of △ | Interior angles of a triangle sum to $180^\circ$ |
| Ext. ∠ of △ | An exterior angle of a triangle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles |
| ∠ sum of □ | Interior angles of a quadrilateral sum to $360^\circ$ |
| ∠ sum of $n$-gon | Interior angles of an $n$-sided polygon sum to $(n-2) \times 180^\circ$ |
| Each int. ∠ of regular $n$-gon | $\dfrac{(n-2) \times 180°}{n}$ |

## Triangles

### Congruence Tests

| Ref | Condition |
|-----|-----------|
| SSS | Three pairs of corresponding sides are equal |
| SAS | Two sides and the included angle are equal |
| ASA | Two angles and the included side are equal |
| AAS | Two angles and a non-included side are equal |
| RHS | Right angle, hypotenuse, and one side are equal |

### Similarity Tests

| Ref | Condition |
|-----|-----------|
| AAA | Three pairs of corresponding angles are equal |
| AA | Two pairs of corresponding angles are equal |
| SSS (ratio) | Three pairs of corresponding sides are in proportion |
| SAS (ratio) | Two pairs of corresponding sides in proportion and included angles equal |

### Triangle Properties

| Ref | Statement |
|-----|-----------|
| Isosceles △ | Base angles are equal |
| Equilateral △ | All angles are $60^\circ$ |
| Midpoint thm. | The segment joining the midpoints of two sides is parallel to the third side and half its length |
| Intercept thm. | A line parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides proportionally |
| Triangle proportionality | $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$ if $DE \parallel BC$ |

## Circles

### Chords & Arcs

| Ref | Statement |
|-----|-----------|
| Perp. from centre bisects chord | The perpendicular from the centre of a circle to a chord bisects the chord |
| Equal chords ⟹ equal arcs | Equal chords subtend equal arcs and vice versa |
| Equal chords equidistant | Equal chords are equidistant from the centre |
| Chords equidistant | Chords equidistant from the centre are equal |

### Angle Properties

| Ref | Statement |
|-----|-----------|
| ∠ at centre = 2× ∠ at circumference | The angle subtended by an arc at the centre is double the angle subtended at any point on the circumference |
| ∠s in same segment | Angles in the same segment of a circle are equal |
| ∠ in semicircle | The angle in a semicircle is $90^\circ$ |
| Converse of ∠ in semicircle | If an angle at the circumference is $90^\circ$, the chord subtending it is a diameter |

### Cyclic Quadrilaterals

| Ref | Statement |
|-----|-----------|
| Opp. ∠s supplementary | Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral sum to $180^\circ$ |
| Ext. ∠ = int. opp. ∠ | An exterior angle of a cyclic quadrilateral equals its interior opposite angle |
| Concyclic test (∠) | If $\angle BAC = \angle BDC$ (same side of $BC$), then $A,B,C,D$ are concyclic |
| Concyclic test (opp. ∠s) | If a pair of opposite angles are supplementary, the quadrilateral is cyclic |
| Concyclic test (ext. ∠) | If an exterior angle equals its interior opposite, the quadrilateral is cyclic |
| Ptolemy's thm. | For cyclic $ABCD$: $AC \cdot BD = AB \cdot CD + AD \cdot BC$ |

### Tangents

| Ref | Statement |
|-----|-----------|
| Tangent ⊥ radius | A tangent is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact |
| Two tangents from ext. point | From an external point, the two tangents are equal in length |
| Tangents subtend equal ∠s at centre | Tangents from an external point subtend equal angles at the centre |
| Line to ext. point bisects ∠ | The line from the centre to the external point bisects the angle between the tangents |
| Alternate segment thm. | The angle between a tangent and a chord equals the angle in the alternate segment |
| Converse of alt. seg. thm. | If the angle between a chord and a line through its endpoint equals the angle in the alternate segment, the line is a tangent |

## Coordinate Geometry

### Straight Lines

| Ref | Statement |
|-----|-----------|
| Slope $= \tan\theta$ | The slope of a line equals the tangent of its inclination |
| Parallel lines | Two non-vertical lines are parallel $\iff$ equal slopes |
| Perpendicular lines | Two lines are perpendicular $\iff$ product of slopes $= -1$ |
| Coincident lines | Two lines are coincident if they have the same slope and same intercept |

### Circles

| Ref | Statement |
|-----|-----------|
| Point inside/on/outside | $P$ is inside/on/outside the circle if $PC < r$, $PC = r$, $PC > r$ respectively |
| Secant | A line cuts a circle at two distinct points $\iff \Delta > 0$ |
| Tangent | A line is tangent to a circle $\iff \Delta = 0$ |
| No intersection | A line does not meet a circle $\iff \Delta < 0$ |

## Loci

| Ref | Statement |
|-----|-----------|
| Fixed dist. from point | Locus of points at fixed distance $r$ from $P$ = circle, centre $P$, radius $r$ |
| Equidistant from two points | Locus = perpendicular bisector of the segment joining them |
| Fixed dist. from line | Locus = two lines parallel to the given line at that distance |
| Equidistant from parallel lines | Locus = line parallel to both, midway between them |
| Equidistant from intersecting lines | Locus = the two angle bisectors of the angles formed |

## Trigonometry

### Identities

| Ref | Statement |
|-----|-----------|
| Odd/even | $\sin(-\theta) = -\sin\theta$, $\cos(-\theta) = \cos\theta$, $\tan(-\theta) = -\tan\theta$ |
| Co-function | $\sin(90°-\theta) = \cos\theta$, $\cos(90°-\theta) = \sin\theta$ |
| Supplementary | $\sin(180°-\theta) = \sin\theta$, $\cos(180°-\theta) = -\cos\theta$ |
| Pythagorean | $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$ |

### Rules

| Ref | Statement |
|-----|-----------|
| Sine rule | $\dfrac{a}{\sin A} = \dfrac{b}{\sin B} = \dfrac{c}{\sin C} = 2R$ |
| Cosine rule | $a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos A$ |
| Area of $\triangle$ | $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}ab\sin C$ |
| Heron's formula | $\text{Area} = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$, $s = \frac{a+b+c}{2}$ |

## Algebra

### Quadratic Equations

| Ref | Statement |
|-----|-----------|
| Discriminant test | $\Delta > 0$: 2 real roots; $\Delta = 0$: 1 repeated root; $\Delta < 0$: no real roots |
| Sum of roots | $\alpha + \beta = -\dfrac{b}{a}$ |
| Product of roots | $\alpha\beta = \dfrac{c}{a}$ |
| Forming eqn. from roots | $(x-\alpha)(x-\beta) = 0 \implies x^2 - (\alpha+\beta)x + \alpha\beta = 0$ |

### Polynomials

| Remainder theorem | Remainder of $f(x) \div (x-a)$ is $f(a)$ |
| Factor theorem | $(x-a)$ is a factor of $f(x) \iff f(a) = 0$ |

## Probability & Counting

### Probability Laws

| Ref | Statement |
|-----|-----------|
| Addition law | $P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)$ |
| Mutually exclusive | $P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B)$ |
| Complementary | $P(A') = 1 - P(A)$ |
| Independent | $P(A \cap B) = P(A) \times P(B)$ |
| Conditional | $P(A \cap B) = P(A) \times P(B \mid A)$ |
| Classical | $P(\text{event}) = \dfrac{\text{favourable}}{\text{total}}$ |

### Counting

| Ref | Statement |
|-----|-----------|
| Addition principle | Mutually exclusive ways → add |
| Multiplication principle | Sequential choices → multiply |
| Permutation | $P^n_r = \dfrac{n!}{(n-r)!}$ |
| Combination | $C^n_r = \dbinom{n}{r} = \dfrac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}$ |
| Relationship | $P^n_r = r! \times C^n_r$ |
