# More about Polynomials

## Division of Polynomials
$$\frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = Q(x) + \frac{R(x)}{g(x)}$$

Division algorithm:
$$\text{Dividend} = \text{Divisor} \times \text{Quotient} + \text{Remainder}$$

Methods: long division or synthetic division.

## Remainder Theorem
When $f(x)$ is divided by $mx-n$, the remainder is $f\!\left(\frac{n}{m}\right)$.

In general, remainder of $f(x) \div (x-a)$ is $f(a)$.

## Factor Theorem
If $f(a)=0$, then $(x-a)$ is a factor of $f(x)$.

Converse: if $(x-a)$ is a factor of $f(x)$, then $f(a)=0$.

Factorise polynomials such as $x^3 \pm a^3$.

## GCD and LCM of Polynomials
- Greatest Common Divisor (gcd / H.C.F.)
- Least Common Multiple (lcm)
