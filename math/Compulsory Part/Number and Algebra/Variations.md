# Variations

## Direct Variation
$y$ varies directly as $x$: $y = kx$, where $k$ is the variation constant.

Graph: straight line through origin with slope $k$.

## Inverse Variation
$y$ varies inversely as $x$: $y = \dfrac{k}{x}$.

Graph: rectangular hyperbola.

## Joint Variation
$y$ varies jointly as $x$ and $z$: $y = kxz$.

Combined forms: $y \propto xz$, $y \propto \frac{x}{z}$, etc.

## Partial Variation
$y$ is partly constant and partly varies as $x$:
$$y = a + bx$$

## Real-life Applications
Problems in physics, economics, and daily life involving proportional relationships.
