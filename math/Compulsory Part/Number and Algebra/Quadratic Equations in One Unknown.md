# Quadratic Equations in One Unknown

## Factor Method
$$ax^2+bx+c=0 \quad (a\neq0)$$

Factorise $ax^2+bx+c$ into $(px+q)(rx+s)=0$, then $x=-\frac{q}{p}$ or $x=-\frac{s}{r}$.

## Forming Quadratic Equations from Roots
If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are roots, the equation is:
$$(x-\alpha)(x-\beta)=0$$
$$x^2-(\alpha+\beta)x+\alpha\beta=0$$

Roots confined to real numbers for Foundation Topics.

## Quadratic Formula
$$x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$$

## Discriminant and Nature of Roots
$$\Delta = b^2-4ac$$

| $\Delta$ | Nature of Roots |
|----------|-----------------|
| $\Delta>0$ | Two distinct real roots |
| $\Delta=0$ | One (double) real root |
| $\Delta<0$ | Two nonreal (complex) roots |

Non-foundation: expressing nonreal roots in the form $a\pm bi$, simplifying surd expressions.

## Sum and Product of Roots
For $ax^2+bx+c=0$ with roots $\alpha$, $\beta$:
$$\alpha+\beta = -\frac{b}{a}, \qquad \alpha\beta = \frac{c}{a}$$

Form equations using these relations.

## Complex Numbers
$$i^2 = -1, \quad i^1=i, \quad i^3=-i, \quad i^4=1$$

Nonreal roots in form $a\pm bi$. Operations: addition, subtraction, multiplication, division of complex numbers confined to $a\pm bi$.
