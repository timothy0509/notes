# More about Equations

## Simultaneous Equations (One Linear, One Quadratic)
Solve $y=mx+c$ and $y=ax^2+bx+c$.

### Graphical Method
Plot both graphs; intersection points are the solutions.

### Algebraic Method
Substitute the linear equation into the quadratic equation, then solve the resulting quadratic.

## Equations Transformable into Quadratic Equations
Types:
- Fractional equations
- Exponential equations (e.g. $4^x-3\cdot2^x-4=0$)
- Logarithmic equations (e.g. $\log(x-22)+\log(x+26)=2$)
- Trigonometric equations (solutions $0^\circ$ to $360^\circ$)
