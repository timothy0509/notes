# Inequalities and Linear Programming

## Compound Linear Inequalities in One Unknown
Solve inequalities involving "and" (intersection) or "or" (union).

Triangle inequalities are also required.

## Quadratic Inequalities in One Unknown

### Graphical Method
Sketch $y=ax^2+bx+c$ and read the $x$-range where the graph satisfies the inequality.

### Algebraic Method
Factorise $ax^2+bx+c$ and consider sign patterns using a table.

| $x$ | $x<\alpha$ | $\alpha<x<\beta$ | $x>\beta$ |
|-----|------------|-------------------|------------|
| $(x-\alpha)(x-\beta)$ | $+$ | $-$ | $+$ |

## Linear Inequalities in Two Unknowns
Graph $ax+by+c>0$ or $<0$: boundary line $ax+by+c=0$ (dashed if strict, solid if $\leq$, $\geq$). Shade the half-plane satisfying the inequality.

## Systems of Linear Inequalities
Intersection of half-planes from multiple inequalities. Feasible region is the overlapping area.

## Linear Programming
Maximise/minimise objective function $P = ax + by$ subject to constraints (system of linear inequalities).

Steps:
1. Graph the feasible region
2. Evaluate $P$ at each vertex of the feasible region
3. Optimal value occurs at a vertex (or along an edge)
