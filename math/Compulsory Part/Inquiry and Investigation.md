# Inquiry and Investigation

Not an independent learning unit. Time is allocated for engaging in learning activities across different learning units.

## Purpose
Through various learning activities, discover and construct knowledge, improving the ability to:
- Inquire
- Communicate
- Reason
- Conceptualise mathematical concepts

## Approach
- Learning activities embedded in other learning units
- Hands-on exploration and discovery
- Problems that require investigation rather than routine application
- Encourages open-mindedness and independent thinking
