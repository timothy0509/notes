## Ptolemy's Theorem
For a cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$:
$$AC \cdot BD = AB \cdot CD + AD \cdot BC$$

## Linear Correlation and Linearisation
Model two data sets showing strong linear correlation.

Reduce non-linear relations to linear form:
- $y = mx + c$ (already linear)
- $y = k a^x$: take logs, $\log y = \log k + x \log a$

## Fibonacci Sequence and Golden Ratio
Fibonacci: $1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, \ldots$

Golden ratio: $\phi = \dfrac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2} \approx 1.618$

Limit of ratio of consecutive Fibonacci terms approaches $\phi$.

## Ceva's Theorem
For triangle $ABC$ with cevians $AD$, $BE$, $CF$ concurrent:
$$\frac{AF}{FB} \cdot \frac{BD}{DC} \cdot \frac{CE}{EA} = 1$$
