# Equations of Straight Lines

## Forms of Equation

| Form | Equation | Remarks |
| ---- | -------- | ------- |
| Point-slope | $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$ | $(x_1, y_1)$ is a point, $m$ is slope |
| Slope-intercept | $y = mx + c$ | $m$ is slope, $c$ is $y$-intercept |
| Two-point | $\displaystyle\frac{y - y_1}{y_2 - y_1} = \frac{x - x_1}{x_2 - x_1}$ | $(x_1, y_1), (x_2, y_2)$ are two points |
| General | $Ax + By + C = 0$ | $A, B \neq 0$ |

Given general form $Ax + By + C = 0$:
$$
\text{slope} = -\frac{A}{B} \quad (B \neq 0)
$$
$$
x\text{-intercept} = -\frac{C}{A} \quad (A \neq 0)
$$
$$
y\text{-intercept} = -\frac{C}{B} \quad (B \neq 0)
$$

See [[Book 4A notes#Chapter 2: Equations of Straight Lines|Book 4A notes]].

## Slope and Inclination

$$
m = \tan\theta
$$

where $\theta$ is the inclination ($0^\circ \leq \theta < 180^\circ$).

## Intersection of Two Lines

Solve the two equations simultaneously. Possible outcomes:
- **One solution** — lines intersect at a point (different slopes)
- **No solution** — lines are parallel (same slope, different intercepts)
- **Infinite solutions** — lines coincide (same line)

## Features of a Line

From the equation, determine:
- **Slope** — direction and steepness
- **Intercepts** — $x$-intercept and $y$-intercept
- Whether a given point lies on the line
