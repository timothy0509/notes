# Loci

A **locus** (plural: **loci**) is the set of all points satisfying a given condition.

## Common Loci

| Condition | Locus | Diagram/Sketch |
| --------- | ----- | -------------- |
| Fixed distance $r$ from a fixed point $P$ | Circle, centre $P$, radius $r$ | $\circ$ |
| Equal distance from two points $A$ and $B$ | Perpendicular bisector of $AB$ | $\mid$ |
| Fixed distance $d$ from a line $L$ | Two lines parallel to $L$, distance $d$ away | $\parallel$ |
| Equal distance from two parallel lines | A line parallel to both, midway between them | $\parallel$ |
| Equal distance from two intersecting lines | The two angle bisectors of the angles formed | $\times$ |

## Algebraic Equations of Simple Loci

| Locus | Equation |
| ----- | -------- |
| Straight line | $y = mx + c$ or $Ax + By + C = 0$ |
| Circle, centre $(h, k)$, radius $r$ | $(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$ |
| Parabola (vertical axis) | $y = ax^2 + bx + c$ |

To find the equation of a locus:
1. Let $P(x, y)$ be a point on the locus
2. Write the condition in terms of $x$ and $y$
3. Simplify to obtain the equation
