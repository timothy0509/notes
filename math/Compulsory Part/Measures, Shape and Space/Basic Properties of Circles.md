# Basic Properties of Circles

## Chords and Arcs

- Equal chords subtend equal arcs
- Equal arcs subtend equal chords
- The perpendicular from the centre to a chord bisects the chord
- The line joining the centre to the midpoint of a chord is perpendicular to the chord
- The perpendicular bisector of a chord passes through the centre
- Equal chords are equidistant from the centre
- Chords equidistant from the centre are equal
- There is **one and only one** circle passing through three non-collinear points

## Angle Properties of a Circle

| Property | Statement |
| -------- | --------- |
| Angle at centre | Angle subtended by an arc at the centre is **double** the angle subtended at any point on the circumference |
| Angles in same segment | Angles in the same segment are equal |
| Angle in semicircle | The angle in a semicircle is a right angle ($90^\circ$) |
| Converse of semicircle | If an angle at the circumference is $90^\circ$, the chord subtending it is a diameter |

Arcs are proportional to their corresponding angles at the circumference.

## Cyclic Quadrilaterals

A quadrilateral is **cyclic** if all four vertices lie on a circle.

- Opposite angles are **supplementary** (sum to $180^\circ$)
- An exterior angle equals its **interior opposite angle**

### Tests for Concyclic Points

A quadrilateral is cyclic if:
- $\angle BAC = \angle BDC$ (points $A, B, C, D$ on same side of $BC$)
- A pair of opposite angles are supplementary
- An exterior angle equals its interior opposite angle

## Tangents to a Circle

- A tangent is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact
- The perpendicular to a tangent at its point of contact passes through the centre
- If a line is perpendicular to a radius at its endpoint on the circle, it is a tangent
- From an external point, the two tangents are **equal** in length
- The tangents subtend equal angles at the centre
- The line from the centre to the external point bisects the angle between the tangents

### Tangent-Chord Angle (Alternate Segment)

The angle between a tangent and a chord equals the angle in the **alternate segment**.

Conversely, if a line through an endpoint of a chord makes an angle with the chord equal to the angle in the alternate segment, the line is a tangent.
