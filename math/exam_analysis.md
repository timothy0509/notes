## Detailed Topic Analysis & Predictions

### 🔴 CERTAIN (appeared all 3 years in both papers)

1. **Algebraic Simplification / Fractions (P1 Q1-2, P2 Q1-5)**
   - Always the easiest questions. Includes fraction operations, indices, rationalizing denominators.
   - **Prediction**: Definitely appears. Practice: fraction addition/subtraction, laws of indices, surds.

2. **Compound Inequalities (P1 Q4, P2 Q7-8)**
   - Always appears as a standard question. Solve and represent on number line.
   - **Prediction**: Definitely appears. Practice: solving compound inequalities, graphing solutions.

3. **Word Problems with Algebra (P1 Q5)**
   - Ratio problems, cost/price problems, age problems.
   - **Prediction**: Definitely appears. Practice: setting up equations from word problems.

4. **Circle Geometry (P1 Q6-8, P2 Q25-27)**
   - Angles in circles, arcs, chords, tangent properties.
   - **Prediction**: Definitely appears. Practice: angle properties in circles (inscribed angle, tangent-chord angle, cyclic quadrilateral).

5. **Statistics (P1 Q7-10, P2 Q17-19, Q33, Q35)**
   - Mean, median, mode, IQR, standard deviation, stem-and-leaf, box-whisker.
   - **Prediction**: Definitely appears. Practice: calculating mean/std dev from frequency distributions, interpreting box-whisker plots.

6. **Probability (P1 Q7-10, P2 Q22, Q30, Q36)**
   - Conditional probability, tree diagrams, independent/dependent events.
   - **Prediction**: Definitely appears. Practice: conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, multi-stage problems.

7. **Variation (P1 Q11-12, P2 Q13-14)**
   - Direct variation, partial variation (y = ax + b, y = ax² + b).
   - **Prediction**: Definitely appears. Practice: identifying type of variation, finding constants.

8. **Polynomial Division (P1 Q12-13, P2 Q9-10, Q12)**
   - Remainder theorem, factor theorem, polynomial long division.
   - **Prediction**: Definitely appears. Practice: synthetic division, applying remainder/factor theorem.

9. **Quadratic Functions (P1 Q16-18, P2 Q11, Q19, Q31)**
   - Vertex form, axis of symmetry, transformation of graphs, discriminant.
   - **Prediction**: Definitely appears. Practice: completing the square, graph transformations, discriminant analysis.

10. **Coordinate Geometry — Circles & Tangents (P1 Q9, Q14, Q17, P2 Q24, Q28, Q32)**
    - Equation of circle, tangent to circle, locus problems.
    - **Prediction**: Definitely appears. Practice: finding circle equations, tangent conditions, locus constructions.

11. **3D Geometry (P1 Q15, Q17, Q19, P2 Q30, Q38, Q43)**
    - Dihedral angles, angle between line and plane, projections.
    - **Prediction**: Definitely appears. Practice: finding dihedral angles using cosine formula, projections.

12. **Sequences (P1 Q8, Q13-14, Q16, P2 Q17, Q20, Q29, Q37)**
    - Arithmetic and geometric sequences, sum formulas, recursive definitions.
    - **Prediction**: Definitely appears. Practice: nth term formulas, sum formulas, recursive sequences.

13. **Mensuration (P1 Q10, Q15, P2 Q21)**
    - Volume and surface area of cone, sphere, cylinder. Similar figures.
    - **Prediction**: Definitely appears. Practice: cone/sphere/cylinder formulas, similar figure scaling.

14. **Function Transformations (P1 Q6, Q16, Q18, P2 Q6, Q9, Q11)**
    - Graph transformations: translation, reflection, stretching.
    - **Prediction**: Definitely appears. Practice: y = f(x) + a, y = f(x+a), y = af(x), y = f(ax).

15. **Logarithms (P2 Q28, Q34, Q38)**
    - Logarithm properties, solving log equations, log graphs.
    - **Prediction**: MC only. Practice: log laws, change of base, solving log equations.

16. **Complex Numbers (P2 Q28, Q33, Q41)**
    - Operations with complex numbers, purely imaginary/real conditions.
    - **Prediction**: MC only. Practice: arithmetic with i, modulus, argument.

17. **Permutations & Combinations (P1 Q15, P2 Q23, Q36-37, Q45)**
    - Basic counting, permutations, combinations, "at least" problems.
    - **Prediction**: Definitely appears. Practice: nPr, nCr, "at least" with complementary counting.

### 🟡 LIKELY (appeared 2 of 3 years)

1. **Similar Triangles / Congruence (P1 Q13)**
   - Proof of similarity, ratio of sides.
   - **Prediction**: Likely. Practice: similarity conditions, ratio calculations.

2. **Cyclic Quadrilaterals (P2 Q28, Q36)**
   - Properties of cyclic quadrilaterals.
   - **Prediction**: Likely in MC. Practice: opposite angles sum to 180°, Ptolemy's theorem.

3. **Polar Coordinates (P2 Q28-29)**
   - Conversion between polar and Cartesian.
   - **Prediction**: Likely in MC. Practice: r = √(x²+y²), θ = arctan(y/x).

4. **Area Problems (P2 Q30, Q37)**
   - Parallelogram, trapezium area calculations.
   - **Prediction**: Likely in MC. Practice: area formulas, coordinate geometry area.

5. **Standard Deviation / Variance (P2 Q36, P1 Q7-10)**
   - Calculating and interpreting spread.
   - **Prediction**: Likely. Practice: formula application, comparison of distributions.

## Key Observations

1. **Paper 1 is highly predictable**: Questions 1-9 follow a consistent pattern every year. The topics rotate within a fixed set.

2. **Paper 2 MC covers the full syllabus**: Every topic appears at least once. Focus on breadth of knowledge.

3. **2024-25 introduced new topics**: Permutations in Paper 1 Q15, trigonometry bearings Q9, binary numbers, orthocentre. These may signal a broader syllabus coverage trend.

4. **Quadratic functions always appear as the hardest Paper 1 question**: Q16-18 consistently involves vertex form, transformations, and geometric applications.

5. **3D geometry is always a high-mark question**: Dihedral angles and projections appear every year in Paper 1.

6. **Statistics questions are getting more complex**: 2024-25 included adjustment problems (adding constant to all values).
