# Second Derivative

## Definition

The second derivative is the derivative of the derivative:

$$f''(x) = \frac{d}{dx} f'(x)$$

## Notations

$$y'', \quad f''(x), \quad \frac{d^2 y}{dx^2}$$

Third and higher order derivatives are **not required**.

## Concavity

| $f''(x)$ | Concavity |
|---|---|
| $f''(x) > 0$ | Concave up (∪) |
| $f''(x) < 0$ | Concave down (∩) |

## Second Derivative Test

For a stationary point $c$ (where $f'(c) = 0$):

| Condition | Conclusion |
|---|---|
| $f''(c) > 0$ | Local minimum |
| $f''(c) < 0$ | Local maximum |
| $f''(c) = 0$ | Test inconclusive |
