# Applications of Differentiation

## Tangent Problems

Equation of tangent to $y = f(x)$ at $x = x_0$:

$$y - f(x_0) = f'(x_0)(x - x_0)$$

## Rate of Change

If $y = f(x)$ and both depend on time $t$:

$$\frac{dy}{dt} = \frac{dy}{dx} \cdot \frac{dx}{dt}$$

## Maximum and Minimum

### Stationary Points

Solve $f'(x) = 0$ to find critical points.

### Local vs Global Extrema

- **Local extremum**: greatest/least value in a neighbourhood
- **Global extremum**: greatest/least value over the entire domain

Test extrema at:
1. Stationary points ($f'(x) = 0$)
2. Endpoints of the domain
3. Points where $f'(x)$ does not exist

Use [[Second Derivative]] test or first derivative test to classify.
