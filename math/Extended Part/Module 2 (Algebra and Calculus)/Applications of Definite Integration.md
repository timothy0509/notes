# Applications of Definite Integration

## Area of Plane Figures

The area bounded by $y = f(x)$, the $x$-axis, $x = a$ and $x = b$:
$$
A = \int_a^b |f(x)|\,dx
$$

Area between two curves $y = f(x)$ and $y = g(x)$ where $f(x) \ge g(x)$ on $[a, b]$:
$$
A = \int_a^b [f(x) - g(x)]\,dx
$$

## Volume of Solids of Revolution

### Disc Method (about x-axis)

Rotating $y = f(x)$ about the $x$-axis from $x = a$ to $x = b$:
$$
V = \pi \int_a^b [f(x)]^2 \,dx
$$

### About y-axis

Rotating $x = g(y)$ about the $y$-axis from $y = c$ to $y = d$:
$$
V = \pi \int_c^d [g(y)]^2 \,dy
$$

### About a Line Parallel to an Axis

Rotating about $y = k$ (horizontal line):
$$
V = \pi \int_a^b [f(x) - k]^2 \,dx
$$

Rotating about $x = h$ (vertical line):
$$
V = \pi \int_c^d [g(y) - h]^2 \,dy
$$
