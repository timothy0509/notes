# Applications of Vectors

## Division of a Line Segment

For points $A$ and $B$, a point $P$ dividing $AB$ in the ratio $m:n$:

$$
\mathbf{OP} = \frac{n\mathbf{OA} + m\mathbf{OB}}{m + n}
$$

- Internal division: $m:n > 0$
- Midpoint: $\mathbf{OP} = \frac{\mathbf{OA} + \mathbf{OB}}{2}$

## Parallelism

Two non-zero vectors are parallel if one is a scalar multiple of the other:
$$
\mathbf{a} \parallel \mathbf{b} \iff \mathbf{a} = \lambda\mathbf{b}
$$

## Orthogonality

Two non-zero vectors are perpendicular (orthogonal) if their dot product is zero:
$$
\mathbf{a} \perp \mathbf{b} \iff \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 0
$$

## Angle Between Vectors

$$
\cos\theta = \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{|\mathbf{a}| |\mathbf{b}|}
$$

## Projection

Scalar projection of $\mathbf{a}$ onto $\mathbf{b}$:
$$
\text{comp}_{\mathbf{b}} \mathbf{a} = \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{|\mathbf{b}|}
$$

Vector projection of $\mathbf{a}$ onto $\mathbf{b}$:
$$
\text{proj}_{\mathbf{b}} \mathbf{a} = \left(\frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{|\mathbf{b}|^2}\right) \mathbf{b}
$$

## Area of Triangle

Given two sides as vectors $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$:
$$
\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} |\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}|
$$
